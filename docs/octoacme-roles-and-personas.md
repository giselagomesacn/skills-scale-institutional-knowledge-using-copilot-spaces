# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect provides technical direction, ensures the solution fits the target architecture, and helps the team make sound implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical design direction and architectural guardrails
- Review solution trade-offs, dependencies, and integration points
- Help break down complex work into implementation-ready tasks
- Partner with developers on technical quality, maintainability, and delivery risk
- Support planning with technology feasibility and implementation constraints

### Goals
- Deliver a technically sound and sustainable solution
- Reduce avoidable implementation risk and rework
- Align engineering decisions with product and project goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and design reviews
- Technical feasibility checks during planning and execution
- Escalation guidance for dependencies, security, and platform concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate business needs into technical constraints and trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by identifying delivery risks, dependencies, and sequencing considerations
- Collaborates with Developers to review designs, standards, and implementation quality
- Coordinates with Quality Assurance to align testability and acceptance expectations

---

## Stakeholder Manager

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Manager ensures that project goals, progress, and trade-offs are communicated clearly to stakeholders and that stakeholder expectations remain aligned to project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder maps, engagement plans, and communication cadence
- Provide status updates and decision context for executive or cross-functional audiences
- Surface concerns, dependencies, and unresolved issues that require stakeholder input
- Align project communication with business priorities and customer impact

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders informed and engaged
- Reduce confusion and decision delays
- Maintain trust through consistent communication and transparency

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder updates and steering committee briefings
- Risk and milestone communication for business audiences
- Status summaries tied to customer and strategic outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to coordinate reporting and escalation paths
- Works with Product Managers to confirm priorities, scope decisions, and customer value statements
- Keeps Developers informed of business context without overloading them with external communication concerns
- Helps align Release Manager and operational teams around launch readiness and business expectations

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead defines the quality bar for the project, manages validation activities, and ensures that deliverables meet agreed standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality criteria, and release readiness checks
- Coordinate functional, regression, and exploratory testing efforts
- Track defects, ensure prioritization, and verify remediation outcomes
- Partner with teams to improve reliability, usability, and operational confidence

### Goals
- Reduce defects and production risk
- Improve confidence in release quality and user experience
- Protect the team from avoidable quality regressions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, bug triage, and quality dashboards
- Release readiness reviews and risk-based quality assessments
- Feedback loops with engineering and product partners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation against acceptance criteria and standards
- Supports Product Managers by clarifying quality expectations and trade-offs
- Collaborates with Project Managers to align test cycles with milestones and release plans
- Coordinates with Release Manager on sign-off criteria for production deployment

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates the end-to-end deployment process, ensures readiness, and reduces operational risk during production changes.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release schedules, communication, and deployment windows
- Validate environment readiness, dependencies, and rollback criteria
- Coordinate launch activities with engineering, QA, and operational teams
- Ensure release documentation and post-release monitoring are complete

### Goals
- Deliver changes with minimal disruption and high confidence
- Reduce deployment-related issues and rollback frequency
- Improve predictability of release outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Release calendars and deployment plans
- Launch readiness reviews and rollback checklists
- Post-release review and operational follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on milestone sequencing and release dependencies
- Coordinates with Quality Assurance for sign-off before deployment
- Works with Developers to confirm deployment readiness and rollback plans
- Aligns with Stakeholder Manager to communicate business impact and timing to impacted audiences

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst connects business goals and user needs to clear requirements, process clarity, and measurable value delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Gather, analyze, and document requirements and business processes
- Clarify scope, assumptions, and success criteria for stakeholders and teams
- Identify gaps, workflow issues, and process improvement opportunities
- Support prioritization decisions with evidence and user-centered analysis

### Goals
- Improve alignment between business goals and delivered outcomes
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and decision-making
- Support effective planning and stakeholder confidence

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Documentation of process flows, user stories, and acceptance criteria
- Decision summaries and trade-off analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine scope and value propositions
- Helps Project Managers and Stakeholder Manager understand planning dependencies and priority changes
- Collaborates with Developers to validate that requirements are clear, testable, and feasible
- Supports Quality Assurance by clarifying expected behavior and validation scenarios

---

## Knowledge Manager / Documentation Owner

### Role Summary
The Knowledge Manager or Documentation Owner maintains the living documentation of process, decisions, and operational knowledge so the team can learn, scale, and execute consistently.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project documentation, training materials, and process references
- Capture lessons learned, decisions, and operational knowledge
- Ensure process docs stay current and aligned with actual team practices
- Help onboard new team members and support institutional knowledge transfer

### Goals
- Improve continuity across teams and projects
- Reduce information silos and recurring confusion
- Strengthen repeatability and scalability of delivery practices

### Typical Communication
- Documentation updates, onboarding guides, and process refreshes
- Lessons-learned sessions and process improvement discussions
- Cross-team knowledge-sharing forums and decision logs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to keep plans, processes, and status artifacts updated
- Supports Product Managers and Stakeholder Manager through clear documentation of priorities and decisions
- Helps Developers and QA teams maintain reusable guidance, standards, and reference material
- Ensures organizational learning is captured for future projects and continuous improvement

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles make it easier to model accountability, collaboration patterns, and handoffs across project teams.
